Code for the manuscript "Glutamate, neurodegeneration, and activation in early Alzheimer's disease: association and mediation" article.
DOI: Manuscript currently under resubmission review.

*The data used in the preparation of this article were obtained from the Consortium for the Early Identification of Alzheimer’s Disease – Quebec (CIMA-Q; cima-q.ca). A full list of researchers involved in the design and development of CIMA-Q is available on the website (cima-q.ca) and in the supplementary materials. Contributions include protocol development, establishment of research infrastructure, participant recruitment and follow-up, data collection, and the collecting, processing and maintenance of biological and ex-vivo samples, as well as the generation of derived data.

## Repository Structure
- `scripts/`: R scripts used for statistical modeling, regression analyses, and sensitivity checks.
- `results/`: Raw Output generated from the analysis pipeline.

## Requirements
- R (version 4.0 or higher)
- Key libraries used:
  - `readxl`
  - `janitor`
  - `jmv`
  - `effectsize`
  - `interactions`
  - `emmeans`
  - `mediation`

## Data Availability
Access to data from the Consortium for the Early Identification of Alzheimer’s Disease (CIMA-Q) requires meeting specific conditions, including CIMA-Q membership, ethical approval from a Quebec research ethics committee (Ministry of Health and Social Services or a university committee), submission of an application to the CIMA-Q User Access Committee, and completion of a data-sharing agreement. Further details on data access procedures are available on the CIMA-Q website: http://www.cima-q.ca/en/data-cima-q/.
The data supporting the findings of this study are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request.
